Sogong-dong (Korean: 소공동) is a dong (neighborhood) of Jung District, Seoul, South Korea.[1]

Economy

[edit]

All Nippon Airways operates the Seoul Office in Room 1501 on the 15th floor of the Center Building in Sogong-dong.[2] Hainan Airlines operates its South Korea office in Suite 1501 of the Samyoung Building in Sogong-dong.[3]
