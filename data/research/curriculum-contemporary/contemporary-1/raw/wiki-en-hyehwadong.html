Hyehwa-dong (Korean: 혜화동) is a dong (neighborhood) of Jongno District, Seoul, South Korea.[1][2] It is adjacent to the Seoul National University Hospital.

Attractions

[edit]
  • PMC Daehangno Jayu Theater - musical Polaroid were played from 3 to 24 August 2008, starring Andy of Shinhwa and former Miss Korea Lee Hanee.[3]

Education

[edit]

Lycée International Xavier was located in this dong. It moved to Gugi-dong in May 2005.[4]
