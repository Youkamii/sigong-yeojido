Theatre in Pyongyang, North Korea

The Moranbong Theatre (Korean: 모란봉극장; Hanja: 牡丹峰劇場) is a theatre located in Moranbong, Pyongyang, North Korea.[1] It was opened in 1946 and renovated in 2006.
